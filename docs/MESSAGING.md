# Fourthform messaging

Fourthform designs and builds custom websites for independent businesses. The client portal gives customers one place to share their brief, review the website, approve the launch and manage everyday content.

## Content order

Explain the offer and intended audience first. Show the value through concrete visitor actions. Use the Mori House concept to demonstrate the design. Explain the four stages, let visitors try the portal, then make the scope and pricing clear.

## Product terms

- Direction: input for the website. Initial Direction is the brief and references. During Review, a Direction describes a specific requested change.
- Revision round: one submitted batch of Directions. Saving drafts and sending Initial Direction use no revision round.
- Stages: Direction, Build, Review, Launch.
- Core: the included toolkit after launch for content updates, basic analytics, search details and domain management.
- Pro: an optional A$39 / month upgrade for scheduled States, deeper analytics and search insights.
- State: a scheduled version of selected website content. Usual content returns when the scheduled period ends.

## Scope and pricing

Site is A$1,500 for up to 5 custom pages and 3 revision rounds. A$200 starts the project; A$1,300 is due on approval. First is A$199 for a business opened within the last six months, with one page, around 6 to 7 sections and one revision round. Both include Core after launch. Additional revision rounds are A$150 each. All prices are in Australian dollars.

## Evidence and tone

Use clear words, specific actions and short paragraphs. Explain a product term when it first matters. Keep the editorial headlines, but support them with plain descriptions. Use no em dashes, fabricated testimonials, invented results or claims of awards.

Mori House is a website concept and example project. The portal is an interactive preview with edits saved on the device. Accounts, team submissions, payments and publishing are simulated. Onboarding carries the visitor’s brief into the Mori House Site example workspace.
