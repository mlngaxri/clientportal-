Initial Direction

Example project brief
01 / Initial directionSubmitted 18 Sep

Restaurant website / Brisbane

Mori House

Make it easy to understand the restaurant, explore the menu and plan a visit.

M
01 / PRIMARY GOAL

Reservations first.

Visitors should reach opening hours and reservation details without searching through the site.

Primary actionReserve a table
02 / AUDIENCE

People planning dinner.

Couples and small groups looking for a calm Japanese restaurant in Brisbane.

03 / PAGES

Three clear destinations.

Home Introduction and atmosphereMenu Dishes and pricingVisit Hours and booking details
04 / VISUAL NOTES

Warm, restrained, tactile.

Timber, evening light, generous spacing and food photography. Keep the typography calm and easy to read.

BRIEF APPROVED FOR BUILD

Menu content and supplied imagery are treated as the source material. Contact and booking details remain illustrative in this demo.
